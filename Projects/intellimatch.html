IntelliMatch

An AI-assisted internship recommendation system designed to connect student profiles with relevant opportunities and make the matching logic easier to understand.

01 / Problem

The challenge

Finding relevant internships can be difficult when opportunities are described differently and students are unsure which roles fit their skills, education, interests, and location. IntelliMatch explores a way to make that discovery process more relevant.

02 / Approach

How I approached it

  • Combine rule-based matching with text similarity rather than relying on a single signal.
  • Use skills, domain or interest, location, and education as profile-matching factors.
  • Use TF-IDF cosine similarity over internship text as a similarity signal, combined with the rule-based score.
  • Show a match percentage, explain why a role may fit, and suggest skill gaps for the student to work on.
  • Collect simple feedback such as Interested and Not Relevant to support future personalization.
03 / Technology

Tools & methods

Next.js · React · Tailwind CSS · PostgreSQL · Python/data preparation concepts · TF-IDF cosine similarity

04 / Reflection

What this project explores

This project connects product thinking with data preparation, matching logic, database-backed application flows, and explainability. It also highlights that recommendations need understandable reasons, not just a score.
